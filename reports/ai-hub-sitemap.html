    Who owns what

    IDG owns Tools & Data. The Academic Development Centre (ADC) and IATL own teaching guidance. Research & Impact Services owns AI for Research. The Centre owns the hub, Learn, Community and Governance. Each page names its owner and a review date in its footer.

    Rules for every page

    • Route people by audience or task, not by org chart
    • Show a "last reviewed" date on each page
    • Every page links back to the tool finder
    • Keep it public: a login only for internal tools

    What already exists at Warwick

    The AI Information Compliance Policy, the AI strategy PDF, ADC's AI pages and the Library's AI guide can be moved or linked into this structure rather than rewritten.
