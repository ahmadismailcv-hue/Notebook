Côte d'Ivoire 570–632 CE

Unrecorded local communities (no known state)

No written records survive for this land in 570–632, and its peoples are known only in general terms. The states later found here came long afterwards: the Senufo founded the kingdom of Kong in the 11th century, and Akan and Anyi peoples migrated in and founded kingdoms much later still. Across Africa, iron smelting, known since the first millennium BCE, was still spreading.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Iron and copper smelting, known in Africa since the first millennium BCE, continues to spread; by c. 1000 it is found across the continent.
