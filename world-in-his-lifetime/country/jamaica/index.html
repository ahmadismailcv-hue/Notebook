Jamaica 570–632 CE

Probably uninhabited until the first Ostionan settlers in the 7th century

Jamaica is believed to have been uninhabited until the 7th century, when it was colonised by Ostionan Ostionoids, probably moving along the south coast of Hispaniola. Known locally as the Little River Complex or 'Redware' people for their red pottery, they lived mainly near the coast and depended heavily on marine resources. Exact dates are uncertain.
