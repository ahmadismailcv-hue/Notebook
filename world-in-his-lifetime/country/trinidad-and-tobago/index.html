Trinidad and Tobago 570–632 CE

Saladoid and Barrancoid-related village societies

Trinidad, near the Orinoco, lay on the route by which Saladoid pottery-making farmers spread from South America into the Caribbean. Saladoid and Barrancoid ceramic traditions are both found in Trinidad and Tobago in the period up to about AD 750, reflecting close ties with the mainland. Dating is approximate.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Villages in Trinidad and Tobago make pottery in the Saladoid and Barrancoid traditions, linked to the South American mainland.
