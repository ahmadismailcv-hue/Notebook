Zimbabwe 570–632 CE

Early farming communities and foragers

Across the eastern half of southern Africa, early farmers left sites with pottery, metal-working, cultivation and livestock. In Zimbabwe, the granite shelters of the Matobo Hills, occupied from the Early Stone Age into historical times, hold an outstanding collection of rock paintings. Great Zimbabwe flourished only from the 11th to the 15th century. Evidence is archaeological.
