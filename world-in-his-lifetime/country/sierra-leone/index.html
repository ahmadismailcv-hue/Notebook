Sierra Leone 570–632 CE

Unrecorded local communities (no known state)

People had lived in what is now Sierra Leone for thousands of years, and the Bulom, Temne and Limba of the coast were probably its earliest inhabitants among present-day peoples. No written records exist for 570–632, and its communities in this period are known only in general terms. Across Africa, iron smelting, known since the first millennium BCE, was still spreading.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Iron and copper smelting, known in Africa since the first millennium BCE, continues to spread; by c. 1000 it is found across the continent.
