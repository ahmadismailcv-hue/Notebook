Sweden 570–632 CE

Svear and Götar chieftains (Vendel period)

This is the Vendel period, named after the boat-grave cemetery at Vendel in Uppland; nearby Valsgärde held unlooted boat graves with rare helmets. The Svear lived around Lake Mälaren and Uppsala, the Götar in Östergötland and Västergötland. At Gamla Uppsala, great mounds and a magnate estate with a hall arose from the late 6th century. Gotland raised carved picture stones. There is no contemporary written history.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Vendel-period boat graves at Valsgärde, near Gamla Uppsala, contain helmets and rich weapons of a warrior elite.
  2. c. 600
    Gamla Uppsala's East and West mounds are built in the late 6th–early 7th century as the site becomes a monumental centre.
  3. c. 600
    Gotlanders carve picture stones in Vendel-period style; the early tradition is dated from the 5th to mid-7th century.
