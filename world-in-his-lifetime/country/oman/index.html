Oman 570–632 CE

Azd and other Arab tribes under the Julanda family, Sasanian vassals; the coast part of the Sasanian province of Mazun

A treaty under Khosrow I gave the Julanda family paramountcy over the Arabs of Oman as tributaries and auxiliaries of Persia, while Persian officials and garrisons held the coast, part of the province of Mazun, and Rustaq inland. After Mecca submitted in 630, the rulers of Oman accepted Islam through Amr ibn al-As, sent by Muhammad ﷺ.

Connection to Arabia The rulers of Oman accepted Islam c. 630 through Amr ibn al-As, envoy of Muhammad ﷺ.
