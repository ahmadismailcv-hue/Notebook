Austria 570–632 CE

Bavarians under Frankish influence; Alemanni in the west; Slavs under Avar rule in the east

After the Lombards left for Italy in 568, the Bavarians, under Frankish political influence, struggled with Slavs invading from the east, while Alemanni settled in today's Vorarlberg. The Slavs had Avar rulers and at their greatest extent reached Lower Austria, Styria and Carinthia. At Bregenz, a former Roman fort where Christianity survived mixed with pagan cults, the Irish monk Columbanus and his companions worked in 610–612.

Timeline

  1. c. 610
    Columbanus and his companions work at Bregenz on Lake Constance (610–612) before he moves on to Italy.
  2. c. 623
    Western Slavs rise against the Avars under Samo (623 or 624); eastern Austria is among the proposed centres of his realm.
