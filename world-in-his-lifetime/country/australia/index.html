Australia 570–632 CE

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples (many distinct nations)

Australia was home to many distinct Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander groups, each with its own culture, language, beliefs and practices. Current research suggests they had lived on the continent for upwards of 60,000 years. Torres Strait Islanders lived on the islands between Cape York and New Guinea. No specific events can be dated for this period.
