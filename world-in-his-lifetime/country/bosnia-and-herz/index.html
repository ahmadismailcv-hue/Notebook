Bosnia and Herz. 570–632 CE

Former Roman province of Dalmatia; Avar pressure and Slav settlement

Most of this land had belonged to the Roman province of Dalmatia, home to Illyrian tribes. After the Avars took Sirmium in 582, Slavs pressed into the Balkans; between the 6th and 8th centuries they settled here and assimilated the Illyrian population. Under Heraclius, Serb groups, earlier Avar vassals, settled inland of the Dalmatian coast, including eastern Herzegovina.
