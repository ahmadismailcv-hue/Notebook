Greenland 570–632 CE

Uninhabited (gap between Early and Late Dorset)

Greenland appears to have been empty of people in these decades. Early Dorset Palaeo-Inuit had lived there until around the start of the common era, then abandoned it; Late Dorset groups returned to the northwest only around 700–800 (sources differ). Meanwhile Dorset people continued to hunt in the Canadian Arctic. Evidence is purely archaeological and dates rest on radiocarbon.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    No dated human occupation in Greenland; Late Dorset Palaeo-Inuit live in the Canadian Arctic and reach northwest Greenland c. 700–800.

Sources

  • Late Dorset (Prehistory of Greenland), National Museum of Denmark
  • Geographic Distribution of the Dorset Culture, The Canadian Encyclopedia
