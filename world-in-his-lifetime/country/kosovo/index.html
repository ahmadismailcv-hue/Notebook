Kosovo 570–632 CE

Eastern Roman Empire (Dardania), giving way to Slav settlement

This was part of the Roman province of Dardania, whose city of Ulpiana Justinian, a native of the region, rebuilt as Justiniana Secunda. Avar and Slav pressure on the Balkans grew from the 580s, and after 602 the Danube frontier crumbled. Archaeologists now question the old view that Avar and Slav attacks quickly emptied Ulpiana. Slavs settled widely in the central Balkans during the 7th century.

Timeline
