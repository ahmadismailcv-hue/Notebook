Antarctica 570–632 CE

No human presence

Antarctica had no native population and no recorded human visits before the 19th century. Rarotongan oral tradition tells of Ui-te-Rangiora sailing south to a frozen region around 650, but the continent itself was first sighted in 1820, by Russian, British or American sailors.
