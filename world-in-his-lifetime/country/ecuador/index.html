Ecuador 570–632 CE

Regional coastal and highland societies (late Regional Development period)

Ecuador had regional societies but no state. On the coast this was the end of the Regional Development period, which lasted until about 700; the Integration period that followed brought population growth and sharper social ranking. Spondylus shell, found off the warm Ecuadorian coast but scarce in Peru's cold waters, had great ritual value in the Central Andes, and Peruvian demand rose sharply after about 600.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Peruvian demand for Ecuadorian Spondylus shell rises considerably after Moche V (c. 600).
  2. c. 600
    In southern Manabí, settlements stay small with little stratification until the end of the Regional Development period (c. 700).
