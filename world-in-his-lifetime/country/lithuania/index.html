Lithuania 570–632 CE

Baltic tribes (no state)

Baltic-speaking ancestors of the Lithuanians kept their pagan religion until the Middle Ages; their written history begins only in the 13th century. Archaeology shows a long tradition of horse burial: in western and central Lithuania people were buried with whole horses or with horse heads and legs. Migration-period (4th–6th century) graves contain many weapons and non-local goods.

Timeline

    Burials with whole horses, or horse heads and legs, continue in western and central Lithuania.
