Niger 570–632 CE

Bura culture of the Niger valley

In the Niger River valley, the Bura culture buried its dead in terracotta funerary jars topped with figures. The Met dates Bura grave markers to the 3rd-11th centuries, and the UNESCO tentative listing places use of the Bura necropolis between the 3rd and 13th centuries. Bura holds the oldest terracotta equestrian figures in the Niger basin. Evidence is archaeological only.
