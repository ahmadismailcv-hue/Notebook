Egypt 570–632 CE

Byzantine Empire; Persian occupation 619–629

Egypt's grain fed Constantinople and Byzantine armies. Its Coptic church was miaphysite and resisted the imperial Chalcedonian church. Persia invaded from 618–619, took Alexandria in June 619 and held Egypt until 629, pillaging monasteries. Amr ibn al-As later led the Arab Muslim conquest of Egypt.

Timeline

  1. c. 619
    Persian forces, invading from 618–619, take Alexandria (c. June 619) and soon control all Egypt.
  2. 626
    Benjamin I becomes Coptic patriarch of Alexandria.
  3. c. 629
    Persian troops withdraw from Alexandria (c. June 629), and Byzantine rule returns.
