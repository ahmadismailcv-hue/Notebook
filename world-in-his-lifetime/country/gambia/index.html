Gambia 570–632 CE

Communities of the Senegambian megalithic zone

Along the Gambia River, communities raised circles of laterite pillars, quarried with iron tools, beside burial mounds, as at Wassu and Kerbatch. Excavated material from the sites suggests dates from the 3rd century BCE to the 16th century CE. Nothing is known of their rulers or languages from writing.
