Iran 570–632 CE

Sasanian Empire (Hormizd IV, Khosrow II, then rapid succession); Kurdish tribes in the Zagros mountains

Iran was the core of the Sasanian Empire, whose state religion was Zoroastrianism, with great fire temples such as Adur Gushnasp. Khosrow II conquered vast Byzantine lands after 603 but lost the war in 622–628 and was killed in 628; Yazdegerd III took the throne in 632. In the Zagros the Kurds were by the end of the Sasanian period a major people, herding sheep and goats; they later fought against the Arab invaders.

Connection to Arabia Abandoned after the fall of Khosrow II, Persian governors in Yemen and Bahrain became Muslims and recognized Muhammad ﷺ (c. 628–632).

Timeline

  1. 590
    The general Bahram Chobin rebels and Hormizd IV is deposed and killed; Khosrow II flees to Byzantine territory.
  2. 591
    With help from the Byzantine emperor Maurice, Khosrow II defeats Bahram Chobin and regains the throne.
  3. c. 624
    Heraclius invades Atropatene and sacks the fire temple of Adur Gushnasp near Ganzak (623 or 624, date disputed).
  4. 628
    Khosrow II is deposed and executed; his son Kavad II makes peace with Byzantium.
  5. 632
    Yazdegerd III, the last Sasanian king, comes to the throne after years of turmoil.
