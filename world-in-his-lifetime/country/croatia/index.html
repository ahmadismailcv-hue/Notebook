Croatia 570–632 CE

Roman province of Dalmatia, collapsing before Avars and Slavs

Roman Dalmatia had its chief city at Salonae, near Split. Avar and Slav pressure on the Balkans grew from the late 500s, and between the 6th and 8th centuries Slavs settled here and assimilated the older Illyrian population. After the Avars sacked the town in 615, its people built a new town inside Diocletian's palace at Split. Heraclius later allowed Croats who accepted Christianity to settle in the northwest Balkans.
