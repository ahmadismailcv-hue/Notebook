Chad 570–632 CE

Sao communities of the Lake Chad basin

The Sao, the oldest known civilisation of the Lake Chad region, settled around the lake by about the 5th century (one UNESCO text says 5th century BC; the date is disputed). They lived mainly from fishing, hunting and farming, left rich pottery, and are regarded as ancestors of the Kotoko. The period 570-632 is known only through archaeology.
