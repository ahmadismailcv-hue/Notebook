Botswana 570–632 CE

Foragers and herders; early iron-using farmers at Tsodilo

At the Tsodilo Hills in the north-west, with over 4,500 rock paintings and a long record of human occupation, the Early Iron Age village of Divuyu dates to the 6th-8th centuries; its people forged iron and worked copper into small ornaments. The nearby site of Nqoma dates to the 7th-10th centuries. No written records exist.
