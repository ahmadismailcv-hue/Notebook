Luxembourg 570–632 CE

Merovingian Franks (Austrasia)

The region lay in Austrasia, the eastern Frankish kingdom, ruled from 561 to 613 by Sigebert I and his descendants. Austrasia was caught up in Merovingian civil wars: in 612 Theuderic II of Burgundy, backed by Brunhild, overthrew Theudebert II of Austrasia, and in 613 Chlothar II became sole ruler of the Franks. In 623 Dagobert I was made king of Austrasia.
