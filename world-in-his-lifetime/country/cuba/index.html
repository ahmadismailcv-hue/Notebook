Cuba 570–632 CE

Archaic (Casimiroid) peoples; Ostionoid farmers beginning to arrive in the east

Cuba had long been home to Archaic (Casimiroid) peoples whose ancestors came from Central America. From about 600, pottery-making Ostionoid farmers expanding from Hispaniola began to colonise eastern Cuba, the start of the line that led to the Taíno. Whether the Ostionoids grew mainly from Saladoid or from Archaic roots is debated. Little can be dated precisely.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Ostionoid peoples expanding through northern Hispaniola begin to colonise eastern Cuba; Archaic communities continue elsewhere on the island.
