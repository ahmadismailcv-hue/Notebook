North Korea 570–632 CE

Goguryeo kingdom (capital Pyongyang)

This land was the heart of Goguryeo, the northernmost of Korea's Three Kingdoms, ruled from Pyongyang since 427. Under King Yeongyang (r. 590–618) it repelled Sui invasions in 598 and 612–614, most famously when general Eulji Mundeok won a great victory at the Salsu River in 612. Many Goguryeo tombs were decorated with painted murals.

Timeline

  1. 598
    A Sui invasion of Goguryeo fails after the army runs short of supplies and meets heavy rains.
  2. 612
    General Eulji Mundeok wins a great victory over a huge Sui army at the Salsu River.
  3. 614
    After a failed attack in 613, a further Sui campaign in 614 is also repelled.
  4. 618
    King Yeongnyu succeeds Yeongyang, reigning until 642.
  5. c. 628
    Goguryeo builds a defensive wall some 480 km long to deter further Chinese invasions.
