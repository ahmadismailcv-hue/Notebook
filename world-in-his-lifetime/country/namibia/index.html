Namibia 570–632 CE

Hunter-gatherers and herders

The drier western half of southern Africa, including Namibia, shows an enduring presence of herders with sheep and some cattle, ahead of and during the spread of farming. Directly dated sheep bones and pottery come from Leopard Cave, and other sites have yielded metal-working and metal artefacts, though their occupants are unclassified. Evidence is archaeological.

Sources

  • Lander & Russell, The archaeological evidence for the appearance of pastoralism and farming in southern Africa (PLoS ONE 13, 2018), PLoS ONE, via PubMed Central
