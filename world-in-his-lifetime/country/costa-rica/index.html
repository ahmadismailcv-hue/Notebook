Costa Rica 570–632 CE

Ranked farming societies of Greater Nicoya, the central and eastern regions, and Greater Chiriquí in the south

Settlements grew in number and size, and rich elite burials show growing social distinctions, with contacts to Mesoamerica and Colombia. Jade carving, a status marker for a thousand years, died out (by about 700) as gold became the preferred prestige material. Polychrome pottery and finely carved effigy metates flourished. Near Arenal, villagers wore deep footpaths walking to distant cemeteries for generations. Evidence is archaeological.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Gold replaces jade as the main prestige material; Maya jade is still traded in and recarved (jade use ends c. 700).
  2. c. 600
    Improved stone carving produces elaborate effigy metates with human, animal and composite figures.
  3. c. 600
    Near Arenal volcano, generations of walking between villages and cemeteries such as Silencio wear footpaths metres deep.
