Dem. Rep. Congo 570–632 CE

Farming and foraging communities; Kamilambian iron-users in the Upemba

Across the Congo rainforest, including the inner Congo basin, radiocarbon evidence shows a population collapse c. 400-600 CE and low activity until c. 1000. In the Upemba Depression in the south-east, the Kamilambian, the area's earliest ironworking culture (c. 6th-8th century), relied on farming and fishing and preceded the Kisalian, which began c. mid-8th century.
