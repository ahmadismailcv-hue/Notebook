Qatar 570–632 CE

Arab communities of eastern Arabia (Bahrain region) within the Sasanian sphere

Qatar lay in eastern Arabia, then called Bahrain (the northeastern Arabian mainland), where Sasanian Persia ruled through Arab allies and a Persian governor (marzban) at Hajar. After Mecca fell to Muhammad ﷺ in 630, the marzban at Hajar converted to Islam and recognized him, as did Persia's Arab protégés in Bahrain.

Connection to Arabia Eastern Arabia's Persian governor at Hajar converted to Islam and recognized Muhammad ﷺ c. 630.
