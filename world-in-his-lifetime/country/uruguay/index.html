Uruguay 570–632 CE

Mound-building groups of the eastern lowlands ('cerritos de indios')

In the Laguna Merín lowlands of eastern Uruguay, people built earthen mounds known as 'cerritos de indios', a tradition dated from about 5,000 to 250 radiocarbon years ago. Older models saw the mound builders as specialised hunters; newer fish-bone evidence suggests fishing was also important. Evidence is archaeological only.

Timeline

    The cerrito mound-building tradition of the Laguna Merín lowlands (c. 5000–250 radiocarbon years BP) continues.
