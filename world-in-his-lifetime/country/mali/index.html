Mali 570–632 CE

Djenne-Djeno and Inland Niger Delta settlements; possible early Ghana (Wagadu)

Djenne, inhabited since about 250 BCE, includes the archaeological site of Djenne-Djeno, part of a UNESCO World Heritage property. Excavations there revealed a pre-Islamic urban settlement of circular and rectangular earthen buildings, funerary jars, pottery and metal slag. To the north-west, the Soninke state of Ghana (Wagadu), dated by Britannica from the 7th century, may have been forming.
