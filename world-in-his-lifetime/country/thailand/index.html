Thailand 570–632 CE

Dvaravati (Mon kingdom) in the lower Chao Phraya valley; other local communities

Central Thailand saw the rise of Dvaravati, the first Mon kingdom in what is now Thailand. Centred in the lower Chao Phraya River valley, it emerged as an independent state late in the 6th century and spread Indian cultural influence. It was rarely politically dominant and lived in the shadow of stronger neighbours. Evidence for this period is limited.
