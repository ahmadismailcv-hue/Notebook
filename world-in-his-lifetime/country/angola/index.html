Angola 570–632 CE

Farming and herding communities (poorly documented)

Angola is thought to be a potentially important route for the spread of herders, farmers and livestock into southern Africa, yet a 2018 survey of the evidence found hardly any archaeology from the region. Pottery from a shell midden at Benfica, on the northern coast, has been proposed as a possible origin of the Kalundu pottery tradition. Little can be said specifically about 570-632.

Sources

  • Lander & Russell, The archaeological evidence for the appearance of pastoralism and farming in southern Africa (PLoS ONE 13, 2018), PLoS ONE, via PubMed Central
