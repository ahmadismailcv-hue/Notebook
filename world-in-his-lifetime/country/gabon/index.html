Gabon 570–632 CE

Forest communities (much reduced)

Iron working had reached Central Africa about 3,000 years ago, but radiocarbon evidence shows a sharp population collapse in Gabon c. 400-600 CE and very low human activity until about 1000 CE. Genetic data suggest that today's Bantu-speaking communities of Gabon arrived only in the second expansion phase (1000-1500 CE).

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Gabon's forest is thinly occupied after a population collapse c. 400-600 CE; resettlement follows from about 1000.
