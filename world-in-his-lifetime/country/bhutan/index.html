Bhutan 570–632 CE

Unknown local societies (no recorded state)

The historical origins of Bhutan are obscure, and little is known of its early history; no written records survive for 570–632. The Bhutia (Ngalops), today's largest group, descend from migrants who came from Tibet starting in the 9th century. Legend says the Tibetan emperor Songtsen Gampo built Kyichu Lhakhang in the Paro valley in the 7th century, but this is tradition, not record.

Timeline

  1. c. 630
    Songtsen Gampo rules Tibet to the north (7th century); legend later credits him with founding Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro.
