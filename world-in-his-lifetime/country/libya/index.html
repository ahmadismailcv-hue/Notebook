Libya 570–632 CE

Byzantine Tripolitania and Cyrenaica; Fezzan, former land of the Garamantes

Justinian's reconquest of 533-534 began with an anti-Vandal revolt in Tripolitania, backed by Roman soldiers from Cyrenaica, and returned the coast to the empire. Inland lay Fezzan, the old territory of the Garamantes, which the Arabs conquered in 666. Arab armies reached the coast in the 640s; excavations show that Tocra in Cyrenaica stayed inhabited after the conquest. Sources for 570-632 itself are scarce.
