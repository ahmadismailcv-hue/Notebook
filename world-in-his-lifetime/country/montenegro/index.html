Montenegro 570–632 CE

Former Roman Illyricum, giving way to Slav settlement

Before the Slavs arrived in the 6th and 7th centuries, the area was inhabited mainly by Illyrians, whom modern Albanians claim as ancestors, and Rome had ruled Illyria for some six centuries. Between the 6th and 8th centuries Slavs settled widely and assimilated Illyrian tribes. Under Heraclius, Serb groups settled inland of the Dalmatian coast, across northern Montenegro.
