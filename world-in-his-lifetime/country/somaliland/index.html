Somaliland 570–632 CE

Gulf of Aden coast (poorly documented)

Seylac (Zeila), on the Gulf of Aden, became the most important Arab settlement on the Somali coast from the 9th century, a centre of trade between Christian Ethiopia and Muslim Arabia in ivory, skins and incense. What this coast was like in 570-632 is not documented in the sources consulted.
