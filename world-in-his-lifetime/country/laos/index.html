Laos 570–632 CE

Early Khmer polities in the Champasak region of the south

Southern Laos, on the Champasak plain beside the Mekong, lay within the early Khmer world; Wat Phu was one of the important Khmer centres of the 7th century. UNESCO dates the planned riverside city of Shrestrapura there to the 5th–7th centuries, and the origins of the Vat Phou temple complex to the 7th century.
