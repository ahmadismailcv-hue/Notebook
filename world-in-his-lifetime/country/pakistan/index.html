Pakistan 570–632 CE

Kingdom of Sindh centred on Aror; Buddhist Gandhara in the northwest

Sindh's early history is known mainly from the Chachnama, a 13th-century Persian translation of a lost Arabic history compiled at Aror. Its local tradition, which mixes history with legend, tells of a Buddhist dynasty overthrown by the Brahman Chach, whose line ruled until the Arab conquest. In Gandhara, the Buddhist monastery of Takht-i-Bahi, founded in the 1st century, remained in use until the 7th century.
