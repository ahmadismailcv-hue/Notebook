Lebanon 570–632 CE

Byzantine Empire, under Persian occupation c. 612–628

The Lebanese coast was part of Byzantine Syria. During the great war Persian armies captured Antioch and pushed through to the Mediterranean coast in 612, then occupied Syria; Byzantium regained the region when Kavad II returned Persia's conquests under the peace of 628.

Timeline

  1. c. 612
    Persian armies take Antioch and push on to the Mediterranean coast during their conquest of Syria.
  2. c. 628
    Under the peace of 628, Persia gives up its conquests and Byzantine authority returns.
