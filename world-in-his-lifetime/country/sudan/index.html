Sudan 570–632 CE

Nubian kingdoms: Nobadia, Makuria and Alodia (Alwa)

Three Christian Nubian kingdoms lined the Nile: Nobadia in the north (capital Pachoras, modern Faras), Makuria (capital Dongola) and Alodia (capital Soba). All converted in the 6th century. Nobadia's ruler adopted Miaphysite (Monophysite) Christianity, while John of Biclaro records Makuria's conversion to the Diophysite faith c. 570. Nobadia and Makuria later merged, sometime in the 7th century or by the early 8th.

Timeline

  1. c. 569
    The missionary Longinus, successor of Julian, begins work in Nubia; by 575 the three Nubian kingdoms have been converted to Christianity.
  2. c. 570
    John of Biclaro records the conversion of Makuria to the Diophysite (Chalcedonian) faith, c. 570.
