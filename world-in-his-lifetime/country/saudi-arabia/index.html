Saudi Arabia 570–632 CE

Independent Arabian tribes and towns (Quraysh of Mecca; Aws, Khazraj and Jewish clans of Yathrib); Muslim community at Medina from 622

The Hijaz had no state. Mecca, home of the Quraysh tribe, was a pilgrimage sanctuary around the Kaaba and a trading town on the caravan route; Yathrib (Medina) was an oasis of Arab and Jewish clans. Muhammad ﷺ, born in Mecca, received his first revelation about 610, emigrated to Medina in 622, entered Mecca in 630, and by his death in 632 had united Arabia under Islam.

Connection to Arabia This was the homeland of Muhammad ﷺ and of the first Muslim community, at Mecca and Medina.

Timeline

  1. c. 570
    Muhammad ﷺ is born in Mecca (c. 570), in the year that, according to tradition, Abraha of South Arabia tried to conquer Mecca.
  2. c. 610
    Muhammad ﷺ receives the first revelation in a cave on Mount Hira near Mecca, at about the age of forty.
  3. 622
    The Hijra: Muhammad ﷺ and his followers emigrate from Mecca to Yathrib (Medina); it marks the beginning of the Muslim calendar.
  4. 624
    In 2 AH the Muslims of Medina win a surprising victory over a Meccan force at Badr.
  5. 630
    In 8 AH Muhammad ﷺ enters Mecca with 10,000 men; the city submits, and he dedicates it to God as the centre of Muslim pilgrimage.
  6. 632
    Muhammad ﷺ dies in Medina (11 AH); Abu Bakr becomes caliph and faces the tribal uprisings known as the Ridda.
