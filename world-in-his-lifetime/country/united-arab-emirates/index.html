United Arab Emirates 570–632 CE

Arab tribes of the wider Oman region, within the Sasanian sphere

This coast belonged to the wider Oman region, where the Julanda family held paramountcy over the Arabs as tributaries of Persia, and Persian garrisons, including one at the tip of the Musandam peninsula, guarded the entrance to the Gulf.

Connection to Arabia As part of the Oman region, its tribes came under the Julanda rulers, who accepted Islam c. 630.

Timeline

  1. c. 630
    The rulers of Oman accept Islam through Amr ibn al-As, envoy of Muhammad ﷺ; Persia's Arab protégés in Oman also recognize him.
