Estonia 570–632 CE

Finnic communities (no state)

The ancestors of the Estonians, a Finnic (Finno-Ugric) people rather than Balts, lived in farming communities during the Middle Iron Age (c. 450–800). Small hillforts appear in the pre-Viking period (c. 600–800), probably housing only a few families and serving as centres of local trade and craft. Weapon hoards, often votive, were deposited at the edges of wetlands. Knowledge comes almost entirely from archaeology.
