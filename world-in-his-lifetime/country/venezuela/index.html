Venezuela 570–632 CE

Village and early chiefdom societies (e.g. western Llanos of Barinas)

There were no states or writing. The best-studied area is Barinas in the western Llanos and Andean piedmont. There, occupation was sparse in 300–550, but in the following phase (550–1000) the number of settlements rose sharply and chiefdoms with centralized authority began to form. Petroglyphs in the piedmont may mark a trade route between the Llanos and the high Andes. Knowledge is entirely archaeological.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    In Barinas, settlements multiply (to 32 in the Llanos and six in the piedmont in 550–1000) and chiefdoms begin to form.
