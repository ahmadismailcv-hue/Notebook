N. Cyprus 570–632 CE

Byzantine Empire (part of Byzantine Cyprus)

The north shared in the life of Byzantine Cyprus and its self-governing church. Its best-known treasure from this period is a set of nine silver plates showing the life of David, made in Constantinople under Emperor Heraclius (stamps dated 613–629/30). They were found together in 1902 in the Kyrenia district and are now divided between the Metropolitan Museum and the Cyprus Museum in Nicosia.

Timeline

    Silver plates showing David's life are made in Constantinople (c. 629–630), possibly commemorating an event of Heraclius's reign; they were later found in northern Cyprus.
