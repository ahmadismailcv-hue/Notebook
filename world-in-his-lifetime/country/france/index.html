France 570–632 CE

Merovingian Franks (Neustria, Austrasia, Burgundy)

Gaul was ruled by Merovingian kings whose kingdoms often fought one another; Guntram of Burgundy tried to keep a balance among his warring relatives. Chlothar II, king of Neustria, became sole ruler of the Franks in 613, and his son Dagobert I ruled the whole realm from 629. Bishop Gregory of Tours wrote his Ten Books of history, and the Irish monk Columbanus founded Annegray and Luxeuil in the Vosges.

Timeline

  1. 592

    Gregory of Tours, bishop and historian, dies (594?).
  3. 613
    Chlothar II becomes sole ruler of the Franks.
  4. 629
    Dagobert I becomes king of all the Franks.
