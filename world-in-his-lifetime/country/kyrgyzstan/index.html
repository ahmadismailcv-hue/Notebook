Kyrgyzstan 570–632 CE

Western Turkic Khaganate (Chu valley)

The Chu valley held Suyab (Ak-Beshim, 60 km east of modern Bishkek), a town probably founded by Sogdians in the 6th century, where Sogdian and local Turkic cultures met. Chinese annals describe the district as densely settled, subject to the Turks and dotted with trading colonies of Sogdian merchants. The Western Turkic khagan Tong Yabghu dominated the region until his assassination.
