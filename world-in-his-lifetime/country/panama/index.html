Panama 570–632 CE

Ranked societies of the Conte phase (central Panama)

Central Panama was in the Conte phase (c. 500–800). Goldworking was fully established, and boldly drawn Conte polychrome pottery showed creatures perhaps linked to myth or cosmology. Elite burials grew richer: the El Hatillo cemetery came into use around 700, and the famous chiefly burial at Sitio Conte dates to about 750. There was no writing; all knowledge comes from excavation.
