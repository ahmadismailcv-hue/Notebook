Cyprus 570–632 CE

Byzantine Empire; autocephalous (self-governing) Church of Cyprus

Cyprus was a Byzantine island whose church had been independent of Antioch since 488. A 6th-century mosaic at Kiti shows close artistic ties to Constantinople. John the Almsgiver, born at Amathus, became Chalcedonian patriarch of Alexandria and died back on Cyprus. Arab raids came later in the 7th century, and in 688 a treaty neutralized the island.

Timeline

  1. c. 610
    John the Almsgiver of Amathus becomes Chalcedonian patriarch of Alexandria (610; some sources say 606), famed for his charity.
  2. c. 619
    With the Persians occupying Alexandria, John flees to his native Cyprus and dies there (619/20; some sources give 616).
