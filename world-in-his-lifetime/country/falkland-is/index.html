Falkland Is. 570–632 CE

Uninhabited (no known settlement)

No settlement is known. Europeans arriving in 1690 found no people. A 2021 study found evidence of pre-European visits by Indigenous South Americans, probably short stays, most likely between about 1275 and 1420, though earlier dates cannot be ruled out. The only native land mammal was the warrah, a fox-like canid.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    No settlement is known; the main evidence for human visits points to c. 1275–1420, centuries later.
