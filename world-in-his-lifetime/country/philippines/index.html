Philippines 570–632 CE

Independent Austronesian communities without central government

The islands were home to diverse peoples with little contact with each other and no centralised government. Later accounts suggest lowland people lived in kin groups called barangays, each led by a datu. In northern Luzon, rice terraces had been built about 2,000 years earlier. Chinese and Indian influence was slight; the first recorded contact with Chinese traders dates to 982. No written records survive from this period.
