Switzerland 570–632 CE

Frankish kingdom of Burgundy (west) and Alamannic lands (north and east)

The west belonged to the Frankish kingdom of Burgundy. Marius, bishop of Avenches from 573, wrote a world chronicle for 455–581 and is credited, without firm proof, with moving the see to Lausanne. To the north and east lay Alamannic lands, where Columbanus and his companions preached around 610–612. His companion Gall stayed behind and built a hermit's cell on the Steinach, the origin of the abbey of Saint Gall.

Timeline

  1. 593
    Marius, bishop of Avenches and author of a chronicle, dies.
  2. c. 612
    Gall, a companion of Columbanus, stays behind and builds a hermit's cell on the Steinach near Arbon.
  3. 613
    Chlothar II becomes sole ruler of the Franks, including Burgundy.
