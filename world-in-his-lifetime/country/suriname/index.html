Suriname 570–632 CE

Village societies of the coast and interior

The record is thin and dates are broad. On the swampy coastal plain of western Suriname, people built raised fields (mounds and ridges with ditches and canals) linked to habitation mounds. According to Rostain, the first raised fields were made by migrants from the lower Orinoco from about 1,800 years ago, and the tradition later spread east into French Guiana. Knowledge is archaeological only.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Raised-field farming, introduced from the lower Orinoco from about 1,800 years ago, is practised on the western coastal plain.

Sources

  • La mise en culture des marécages littoraux de Guyane à la période précolombienne récente (Stéphen Rostain), IRD (ORSTOM) documentation
