Burkina Faso 570–632 CE

Iron-working farming communities

Iron production in the region was very old: Douroula, part of a UNESCO World Heritage property, dates back to the 8th century BCE. The property's other furnace sites show intensified production in the second millennium CE. Little is specifically dated to c. 570-632.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Iron smelting, attested at Douroula since the 8th century BCE, is long established; sites of this era are poorly dated.

Sources
