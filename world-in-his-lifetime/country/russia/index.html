Russia 570–632 CE

Western Turkic power in the south, from which the Khazars later emerged; Kubrat's Bulgars (c. 635)

The southern steppe and north Caucasus lay under the Western Turks. Later texts call them Khazars, but the Khazars become distinguishable from their Western Turkic masters only by the 650s. In 627–628 Western Turkish forces allied with Heraclius stormed Darband and besieged Tiflis. As Western Turkish rule broke up, the Bulgar ruler Kubrat led a successful revolt about 635 and organized the confederation called Great Bulgaria.
