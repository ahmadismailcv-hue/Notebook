Moldova 570–632 CE

Slavic communities within the range of the Antes, under Avar pressure

Slavs arrived in this land between the Prut and Dniester in the 6th century, though invasions disrupted their settlement. The Antes, an eastern Slavic tribal alliance centred between the Dnieper and Dniester, at times extended across the forest-steppe to the lower Danube. The Avars passed through Antes lands about 560, and from the late 500s the Antes fought them stubbornly.

Timeline

    Wars with the Avars break up the Antes alliance; Byzantine writers stop mentioning the Antes in the early 7th century.
