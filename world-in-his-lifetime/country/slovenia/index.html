Slovenia 570–632 CE

Alpine Slavs under Avar overlordship

Slavs, ancestors of the Slovenes, settled here in the 500s and over the following centuries assimilated the older Illyrian population. The Slavs who pressed into the eastern Alps lived under Avar rulers and struggled with the Bavarians to the west. From about 623 a Slavic kingdom under Samo emerged that extended from the Sava valley northward.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Slavs under Avar rulers spread into the eastern Alps, at their greatest extent reaching Carinthia, Styria and Lower Austria.
  2. c. 623
    Samo's Slavic kingdom forms, reportedly extending from the Sava valley northward.
