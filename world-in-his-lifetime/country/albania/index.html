Albania 570–632 CE

Eastern Roman (Byzantine) Empire

Part of the Byzantine Empire, with Dyrrachium (Durrës), long the chief port of Roman Illyricum. The Avars took Sirmium in 582, and Slavs pushed into the Balkans; between the 6th and 8th centuries they settled and assimilated Illyrian tribes farther north, but the tribes of southern Illyria, including modern Albania, kept their own language. The large Komani cemetery (6th–8th centuries) attests a continuing local population.

Timeline

  1. 582
    The Avars capture Sirmium, a Balkan fortress; Slavs who besiege Sirmium and Singidunum in the 580s then push deep into the Balkans.
  2. c. 600
    The large Komani cemetery in the north (6th–8th centuries) shows a local population persisting through the period.
  3. 602
    A mutiny topples Emperor Maurice; afterwards the Byzantine Danube frontier crumbles and is not restored for centuries.
