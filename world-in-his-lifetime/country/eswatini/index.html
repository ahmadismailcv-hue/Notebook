eSwatini 570–632 CE

Iron-using farmers and San foragers (poorly documented)

Iron-using farmers, ancestors of South Africa's Bantu-speaking majority, had settled South Africa from about 200 CE, and decorated pottery and iron-working appear along the coast from southern Mozambique to KwaZulu-Natal. San hunter-gatherers lived in the nearby Drakensberg. No site in eSwatini itself is discussed in the sources consulted.
