Central African Rep. 570–632 CE

Savanna and forest communities (poorly documented)

No written sources exist. Across the Congo rainforest to the south, radiocarbon evidence shows a widespread population collapse between c. 400 and 600 CE, followed by markedly low human activity until about 1000 CE; only 61 sites in the whole rainforest have well-dated evidence from 600-1000 CE.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    In the Congo rainforest to the south, human activity is very low after a collapse c. 400-600 CE.

Sources

  • Seidensticker et al., Population collapse in Congo rainforest from 400 CE urges reassessment of the Bantu Expansion (Science Advances 7, 2021), Science Advances (AAAS)
