Turkmenistan 570–632 CE

Sasanian Empire (Merv, in Khorasan)

Merv, an oasis city on the Silk Road, was one of the main population centres of Sasanian Khorasan; its walled city of Gyaur Kala contained a Buddhist stupa and monastery. Khorasan faced repeated pressure from the Western Turks and their Hephthalite subjects, and after 579 the Sasanians lost control of Tokharistan to the east.

Timeline

  1. 588
    Bahram Chobin defeats a Turk-backed Hephthalite invasion of the eastern provinces that had reached Badghis and Herat.
  2. 590
    Bahram Chobin rebels and is proclaimed king after nobles kill Hormizd IV; Khosrow II, backed by a Byzantine army, ousts him in 591.
  3. c. 615
    Late in his career (he died 616 or 617), the Armenian prince Smbat Bagratuni leads a Persian and Armenian army to victory over the Hephthalites.
