Nigeria 570–632 CE

Farming communities and the early town of Ife; Nok culture long ended

The Nok terracotta culture (c. 500 BCE–200 CE), one of Africa's earliest ironworking centres, had ended centuries before. Ife in the southwest, occupied since c. 350, was growing into an important urban centre in its Archaic period (to c. 800). Near Lake Chad, settlement mounds such as Daima record long-lived Iron Age farming villages. Igbo-Ukwu's famous copper-alloy castings date to about the 10th century, after this period.
