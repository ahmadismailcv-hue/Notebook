Vanuatu 570–632 CE

Independent island communities

Archaeological evidence shows that by 1300 BCE islands in northern Vanuatu had been settled by people of the Lapita culture from Melanesian islands to the west, followed by successive waves of migrants. A highly stratified society developed in central Vanuatu only around 1200. There are no written records for this period.
