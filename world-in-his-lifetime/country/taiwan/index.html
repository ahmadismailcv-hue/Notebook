Taiwan 570–632 CE

Independent Indigenous (Austronesian-speaking) peoples

Taiwan was home to Indigenous peoples whose ancestors had lived on the island for more than 10,000 years and who spoke Austronesian languages. Many scholars see Taiwan as the starting point of the Austronesian expansion, though this is debated. There was no state and no outside rule. Chinese records report an expedition in the early 7th century that brought Indigenous people back to China. Documentation is very thin.
