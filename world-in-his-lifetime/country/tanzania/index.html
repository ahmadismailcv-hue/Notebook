Tanzania 570–632 CE

Early coastal trading villages

Early coastal villages were forerunners of Swahili culture. On Zanzibar, Unguja Ukuu was settled from the 7th, if not the 6th, century and was tied to Indian Ocean trade from its earliest levels. On Pemba, the trading village of Tumbe dates to c. 600-950 and may have produced shell beads for export. Evidence is archaeological.
