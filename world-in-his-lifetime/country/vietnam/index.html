Vietnam 570–632 CE

Annam region in the north; Champa kingdom; Khmer lands in the Mekong delta

In the early 7th century Sui armies campaigned in the region, taking territory from Annam and from the kingdom of Champa; they defeated war elephants with crossbows, but many northern soldiers died of malaria. The Mekong delta in the far south lay within the Khmer world.
