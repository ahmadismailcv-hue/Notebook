Mozambique 570–632 CE

Farming communities; coastal trading sites

Farming communities with decorated pottery and iron-working had settled the coast of southern Mozambique in earlier centuries. In the later first millennium, the beach site of Chibuene was a landing place for Indian Ocean goods; its community supplied imported beads to interior sites such as Schroda. Evidence is archaeological.

Sources

  • Sinclair, Ekblom and Wood, Trade and society on the south-east African coast in the later first millennium AD: the case of Chibuene (2012), Antiquity (Cambridge University Press)
  • Lander & Russell, The archaeological evidence for the appearance of pastoralism and farming in southern Africa (PLoS ONE 13, 2018), PLoS ONE, via PubMed Central
