Paraguay 570–632 CE

Early Guaraní forager-horticulturalists (Upper Paraná)

Guaraní-ancestral groups, originating in southwestern Amazonia, were beginning to settle the Plata basin; the earliest Guaraní sites there date to about 500 ± 100 CE along the Upper Paraná River. They hunted, fished, gathered and grew maize, tubers, beans, squash and peanuts, made corrugated and painted pottery and often buried the dead in urns. Precise dates for this period are few.
