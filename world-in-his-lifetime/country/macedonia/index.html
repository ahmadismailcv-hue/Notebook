Macedonia 570–632 CE

Eastern Roman Empire, with growing Slav settlement

This upper Vardar region lay in the Roman provinces of Macedonia. Stobi, an episcopal seat and capital of Macedonia Secunda, was abandoned in the last decade of the 6th century. Avar and Slav raids hit the Balkans from the 580s, and many Slavs settled here; those of Macedonia kept their Slavic speech. The empire held Thessalonica to the south.
