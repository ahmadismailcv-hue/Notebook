Nicaragua 570–632 CE

Farming communities of the Greater Nicoya cultural area (Pacific side)

There were no states or writing, and everything known comes from archaeology. Pacific Nicaragua belonged to the Greater Nicoya cultural area. During the Bagaces period its potters made Tola Trichrome, painted pottery whose designs are closely linked to Carrillo Polychrome from neighbouring Costa Rica. The Caribbean side is little known.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Bagaces period: potters in Pacific Nicaragua produce Tola Trichrome painted pottery, linked in style to Costa Rican wares.
