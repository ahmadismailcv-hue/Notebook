Fr. S. Antarctic Lands 570–632 CE

No human presence

The Kerguelen and Crozet islands in the southern Indian Ocean had no human inhabitants. They were unknown until French navigators discovered them in 1772: Kerguelen-Trémarec found the Kerguelen Islands and Marion-Dufresne found the Crozet Islands.
