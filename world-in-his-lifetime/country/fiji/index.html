Fiji 570–632 CE

Independent island communities

Fiji's first settlers arrived from other Pacific islands at least 3,500 years ago; Lapita people had spread into West Polynesia from about 1000 BCE. Their descendants lived in island communities with no written records, and the period is known only through archaeology.
