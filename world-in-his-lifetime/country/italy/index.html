Italy 570–632 CE

Lombard kingdom and duchies; Byzantine Exarchate of Ravenna; papal Rome

Italy was divided after the Lombards invaded in 568. They soon held most of the peninsula, but many small dukedoms grew up, while Rome, Ravenna and a few other fortified cities resisted and stayed with Byzantium. Pope Gregory I (590–604) defended Rome against the Lombards and sent Augustine to England. The Irish monk Columbanus went to the Lombard king Agilulf and founded the monastery of Bobbio.

Timeline
