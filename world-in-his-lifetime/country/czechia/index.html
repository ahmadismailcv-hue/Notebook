Czechia 570–632 CE

Slavic tribes; probably part of Samo's realm (c. 623–658)

From the 6th century an early Slavic culture marked by Prague-type pottery spread across this region; ancient DNA from neighbouring Moravia shows a strong population shift between the 5th and 7th centuries. Many Slavs lived under Avar domination. In 623 or 624 a Frankish merchant named Samo was chosen king by Slavs (Wends) who rebelled against the Avars; he ruled until 658, and his realm included at least part of Bohemia. The main source is the Chronicle of Fredegar.
