Norway 570–632 CE

Local elites, no known central authority (Merovingian period, c. 550–800)

Norwegian archaeologists call c. 550–800 the Merovingian period, a time of marked change in burial rites, language, art style and weaponry. Relatively few finds survive from it. Large burial mounds served as symbols of power and wealth, and the first Scandinavian ship burials appear early in the period. There is no contemporary written history, so knowledge is archaeological.
