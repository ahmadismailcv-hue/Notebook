United Kingdom 570–632 CE

Anglo-Saxon kingdoms (Kent, East Anglia, Northumbria and others); Christian Britons in Wales; Iona in the west

England was divided among Anglo-Saxon kingdoms, while Christian churches in Wales predated the Roman mission and Columba's monastery of Iona worked to convert Scotland. Pope Gregory I sent Augustine to Kent in 597, where King Æthelberht received him and was soon baptized. Æthelfrith, king of Bernicia from 592/593, also ruled Deira, together forming Northumbria. The Sutton Hoo ship burial (early 7th century) may be that of Rædwald of East Anglia.

Timeline

  1. 597
    Augustine's mission, sent by Pope Gregory I, reaches Kent and is received by King Æthelberht.
  2. c. 616
    Æthelfrith, king of Bernicia and Deira (Northumbria), dies (date uncertain).
  3. c. 625
    The Sutton Hoo ship burial is made (coins point to c. 610–635); Rædwald is a popular but unproven candidate.
