Jordan 570–632 CE

Byzantine Empire, with Ghassanid Arab allies; Persian occupation c. 614–628

This land lay in Byzantine provinces whose desert edge was guarded by Arab allies, chiefly the Ghassanids, who protected the spice route from southern Arabia. Persia held the region during the great war. In 630 Muhammad ﷺ campaigned to the Syrian border at Tabuk, where various towns submitted to him, and in 636 the Byzantine defeat at the Yarmuk River opened the region to the Muslims.

Connection to Arabia In 630 Muhammad ﷺ led a campaign to Tabuk on the Syrian frontier, where various towns submitted to him.
