Somalia 570–632 CE

Unknown (poorly documented)

The sources consulted say little about the Somali coast in this period. Britannica describes Seylac (Zeila), on the Gulf of Aden in the far north-west, as the most important Arab settlement on the Somali coast only from the 9th century, trading between Christian Ethiopia and Muslim Arabia. Evidence for 570-632 is very limited.

Sources

  • Seylac, Encyclopaedia Britannica
