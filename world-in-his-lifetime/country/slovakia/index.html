Slovakia 570–632 CE

Slavs under Avar rule; possibly part of Samo's realm (c. 623–658)

By the 6th century Slavs lived between the Avar Khaganate in the Carpathian Basin and the Frankish kingdom, many under Avar domination. In 623 or 624 Samo, a Frankish merchant, became king of Slavs (Wends) who had revolted against the Avars; Slovakia is among the proposed centres of his realm. Ancient DNA from neighbouring Moravia points to a population shift linked with Slavic expansion.
