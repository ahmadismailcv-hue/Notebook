Romania 570–632 CE

Slavs and Avars north of the Danube; Byzantine frontier on the river

North of the lower Danube lived Slavic groups, many subject to the Avars, who raided and then settled south of the river. Emperor Maurice campaigned constantly in the Balkans and forced the Avars back across the Danube by 602, but that year his order for soldiers to winter in enemy lands beyond the river caused a mutiny that toppled him. After 602 the Danube frontier crumbled.
