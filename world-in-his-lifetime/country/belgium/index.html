Belgium 570–632 CE

Merovingian Franks (Austrasia and Neustria)

The region lay in the Frankish lands: Austrasia, the eastern Frankish kingdom, covered present-day Belgium among other areas, while Neustria lay to the west. The Merovingian kingdoms fought one another until Chlothar II, king of Neustria, became sole ruler of the Franks in 613, and his son Dagobert I ruled the whole realm from 629.

Timeline

  1. 584
    Chilperic I is assassinated; his infant son Chlothar II succeeds, protected by his mother Fredegund.
  2. 613
    Chlothar II becomes sole ruler of the Franks.
  3. 629
    Dagobert I becomes king of all the Franks.
