Cambodia 570–632 CE

Chenla, an early Khmer kingdom (capital Ishanapura)

By the 7th century Khmer people lived along the Mekong, divided among warring kingdoms whose art and culture were strongly influenced by India. Sambor Prei Kuk, identified as Ishanapura, was the capital of Chenla, which flourished in the late 6th and early 7th centuries. Its brick temples, Sanskrit and Old Khmer inscriptions and sculpture laid foundations for later Khmer art.
