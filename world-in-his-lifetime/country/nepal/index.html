Nepal 570–632 CE

Licchavi kingdom of the Kathmandu Valley

The Kathmandu Valley was ruled by the Licchavi dynasty (c. 450–750), of Indian origin, which used Sanskrit as its court language and issued Indian-style coins. It kept close ties with India and had economic and political relations with Tibet, making Nepal a cultural link between central and southern Asia. The Harsha era beginning in 606 was long used in Nepal.
