Senegal 570–632 CE

Communities of the Senegambian megalithic zone

Communities in the Gambia river region raised circles of laterite pillars, quarried with iron tools, beside burial mounds, as at Sine Ngayene and Wanar. Excavated material suggests dates from the 3rd century BCE to the 16th century CE. No written sources describe them.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    The Senegambian stone-circle tradition is in use at sites such as Sine Ngayene and Wanar.

Sources
