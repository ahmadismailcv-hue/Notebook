Netherlands 570–632 CE

Frisians on the coast; Franks to the south

The Frisians, a Germanic seafaring people, held the coastlands from the Rhine to the Ems. The Franks, centred on the lower Rhine, conquered them only gradually, completing the conquest in the 9th century, so in this period the Frisian coast lay outside Frankish rule.
