Kenya 570–632 CE

Coastal and hinterland communities of the early Tana Tradition

From about 600 CE, sites across eastern Africa, including the Kenyan coast, share locally made pottery known as the early Tana Tradition or Triangular-Incised Ware. Its recognition has led archaeologists to stress the indigenous roots of the later cosmopolitan Swahili coastal culture. Evidence for the period is archaeological.
