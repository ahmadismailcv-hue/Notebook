Azerbaijan 570–632 CE

Caucasian Albania (Aghvank) under Sasanian rule, with the Mihrakan princes

This land was Caucasian Albania, a Christian land under Persian rule with its capital at Partaw (Barda); it is distinct from Atropatene (Iranian Azerbaijan) to the south. Darband and Partaw were key Persian strongholds against northern raiders. Heraclius wintered here in 624, and Turkic allies of Byzantium, called Khazars in the sources, invaded in 626–628. Varaz Grigor of the Mihrakan family then became prince.

Timeline

  1. 624
    Heraclius winters his army in Caucasian Albania after striking into Persian Atropatene.
  2. c. 626
    Khazar allies of Heraclius break through the Darband pass, and the Persian governor of Albania flees.
  3. c. 628
    A second Khazar invasion: their leader declares himself lord of Albania.
  4. c. 630
    Varaz Grigor of the Mihrakan family is consecrated prince of Albania (c. 629–630).
