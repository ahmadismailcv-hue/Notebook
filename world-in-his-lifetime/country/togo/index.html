Togo 570–632 CE

Iron-working farming communities

In the Bassar region of the north, an iron production industry flourished from as early as the Early Iron Age; the large village of Dekpassanware worked iron through the Early and Later Iron Ages. Production grew rapidly in the Later Iron Age, feeding regional trade. Written sources do not exist, and much of the period is undated.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Bassar communities continue an iron-working tradition begun in the Early Iron Age; large-scale production comes later.

Sources
