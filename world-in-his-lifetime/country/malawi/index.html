Malawi 570–632 CE

BaTwa foragers and Iron Age farmers

BaTwa hunter-gatherers, present since the Late Stone Age, painted rock shelters such as those at Chongoni, the richest concentration of rock art in Central Africa. Farming peoples also made rock art there; the Chewa, whose ancestors lived in the area from the Iron Age, painted until the 20th century. Specific evidence for 570-632 is scarce.

Sources

  • Chongoni Rock-Art Area, UNESCO World Heritage Centre
