Belize 570–632 CE

Maya kingdom of Caracol, ally of the Kaanul (Snake) kingdom

Caracol was a rising Maya kingdom. Its inscriptions record the 562 'star war' defeat of Tikal, though the damaged victor's name on Caracol Altar 21 may be that of a Kaanul (Snake) king. Under K'an II, who ruled 618–658, Caracol repeatedly attacked Naranjo. Archaeology shows prosperity and rapid population growth at the start of the Late Classic period, when most of Caracol's causeways were built.

Timeline

  1. c. 599
    Caracol Stela 6 records the seating of a figure called Flame Ajaw for a bloodletting rite, during an interregnum before K'an II's accession.
  2. 618
    K'an II, son of Yajaw Te' K'inich II, becomes king of Caracol; he rules until his death in 658.
  3. 626
    K'an II's forces make the first of three attacks on Naranjo (626–627), recorded at both Naranjo and Caracol.
  4. 631
    Caracol under K'an II defeats Naranjo in a 'star war', probably bringing it under his direct control.
