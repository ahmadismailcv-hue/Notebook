Iceland 570–632 CE

Uninhabited

Iceland had no settled human population in these decades. Britannica notes that Iceland apparently has no prehistory: the oldest written source, Íslendingabók (c. 1130), places Norse settlement in about 870–930, some two and a half centuries after this period.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Iceland remains uninhabited; Norse settlement begins only around 870.
