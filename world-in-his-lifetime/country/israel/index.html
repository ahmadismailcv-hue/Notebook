Israel 570–632 CE

Byzantine Empire (Palestine), under Persian occupation 614–c. 628

This land was part of Byzantine Palestine, with Christian and Jewish communities. When Persia took Jerusalem in 614, Jews were allowed to settle in the city, but this was reversed within two years under Christian pressure. Persia placed Palestine, with its desert frontage, under direct rule in 616 and gave up its conquests under the peace of 628.

Timeline

  1. 614
    Persian forces under Shahrbaraz conquer Palestine and capture Jerusalem.
  2. c. 616
    Persia places Palestine under direct rule, having reversed its permission for Jews to settle in Jerusalem.
  3. c. 628
    Persia gives up its conquests under the peace of 628, and Byzantine rule returns.
