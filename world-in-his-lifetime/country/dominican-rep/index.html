Dominican Rep. 570–632 CE

Ostionoid farming villages and Archaic (Casimiroid) peoples

Eastern Hispaniola was the western limit of the earlier Saladoid expansion, whose advance stalled there, perhaps because of established Archaic populations. By about 600, Ostionoid farmers with simpler red-slipped pottery were expanding across Hispaniola. These communities were ancestors of the Taíno whom the Spanish met. Whether they grew from Saladoid or Archaic roots is debated.

Timeline

  1. c. 600
    Ostionoid farming communities spread through Hispaniola from the east, beside older Archaic groups; Ostionoid origins are debated.
