Kazakhstan 570–632 CE

Western Turkic Khaganate

The steppes belonged to the Turkic (Kök Türk) empire founded in 552, which split into eastern and western halves in 583. Sogdian merchants gave the Turks their first chancellery language and, between 568 and 575, persuaded the khagan to open contact with Byzantium to sell silk. Early in the 7th century the Western Turks under Tong Yabghu extended their rule over the oasis states.

Timeline

  1. c. 568
    Sogdian merchants persuade the Turkic khagan to open contact with Byzantium to sell Chinese silk (between 568 and 575).
  2. 583
    Rivalries within the ruling dynasty split the Turkic khaganate into eastern and western halves.
  3. 627
    Ziebel, brother of the supreme khagan Tong Yabghu, allies with Emperor Heraclius in the Caucasus and conquers Tiflis (627–628).
  4. c. 628
    Tong Yabghu is assassinated (628 or 630), and power struggles weaken the Western Turks' grip on the oasis states.
