Indonesia 570–632 CE

Small Indianised Hindu-Buddhist kingdoms on Java and Sumatra; many local communities

Hindu-Buddhist kingdoms based on Indian ideas of the state had arisen in Sumatra and, somewhat later, Java. Tarumanegara, established about the 5th century, is the oldest recorded kingdom of west Java. Srivijaya, the great Sumatran maritime power based at Palembang, flourished from the 7th century; it is first recorded when the Chinese pilgrim I-ching visited in 671, after this period. Evidence for these decades is thin.
