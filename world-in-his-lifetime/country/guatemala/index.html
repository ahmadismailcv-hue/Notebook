Guatemala 570–632 CE

Maya kingdoms (Tikal, Naranjo and others) under pressure from the Kaanul (Snake) kingdom and Caracol

The Petén lowlands were divided among rival Maya kingdoms that recorded dynastic history in hieroglyphic inscriptions. Tikal's king Wak Chan K'awiil suffered a 'star war' defeat in 562, after which no monuments are known at Tikal for about 130 years. The Kaanul (Snake) kingdom and its ally Caracol dominated the region; Caracol attacked Naranjo from 626, and Naranjo fell in 631.

Timeline

  1. c. 575
    Tikal's long monument hiatus continues after its 562 defeat: no monuments are known there for about 130 years.
  2. 625
    B'ajlaj Chan K'awiil, a Tikal prince who later rules from Dos Pilas and submits to Calakmul, is born at Tikal.
  3. 626
    Caracol's king K'an II attacks Naranjo, the first of three assaults recorded for 626–627.
  4. 631
    Naranjo is conquered; inscriptions credit Caracol's K'an II with a 'star war' and the Kaanul lord Yuknoom Head, aided by fighters from Calakmul, with its seizure.
