Morocco 570–632 CE

Local Romano-Berber communities outside imperial rule

The Roman army had abandoned the southern part of Mauretania Tingitana, including Volubilis, after Diocletian's reorganisation of 285. At Volubilis a cemetery near the Arch of Caracalla has yielded Christian Latin funerary inscriptions dated 599-655 CE, showing a Christianised Romano-Berber population that still used Latin. Islamisation was under way there before Idris I arrived in the late 8th century.
